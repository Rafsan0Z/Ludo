Game of Ludo 

Web Version of the game

Rules of the game:

Consists of 2-4 players, each player having upto 6 pieces in their home base

Each player must roll a 6 on a six-sided dye to remove one piece from the home base. Every time a 6 is rolled a player is allowed to roll again until they do not roll a 6

Each piece must travel around the board and compelete 1 revolution before they can attempt to enter the target base

A player must enter all of their pieces into the target base to complete the game
